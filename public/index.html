Our story

Room to grow.
Space to belong.

Sometimes, the smallest things make a meaningful difference. A seed planted. A skill shared. A conversation in the sunshine.

Leafing Well is being developed as a South Australian community wellbeing initiative and proposed not-for-profit. Our aim is to make gardening accessible to people of all ages, backgrounds and abilities.

We’re creating opportunities to connect with nature, learn new skills, develop friendships and enjoy gentle physical activity — with a little space away from everyday pressures.

Why gardening?

A little nature.
So many possibilities.

You don’t need a big garden or years of experience. There’s joy in slowing down, getting your hands in the soil and seeing what grows.

01

Connection with nature

Notice the seasons, feel the soil and enjoy a little more of the outdoors.

02

Social connection & friendships

Share a task, swap a story and find common ground with others.

03

Relaxation & mindfulness

Slow down and focus on something simple, one small task at a time.

04

Confidence & achievement

Enjoy the satisfaction of trying something new and watching it grow.

05

Gentle movement & meaningful activity

Get moving in ways that suit you, with a purpose you can see.

06

Learning & creativity

Explore new ideas, follow your curiosity and get a little creative.
