Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Leafing Well is a community wellbeing initiative being developed in South Australia. This notice explains how enquiry information will be handled as the initiative takes shape.

Our pre-launch enquiry form

While our enquiry form is marked as a preview, it is disabled and cannot collect or send information. The following explains the intended handling of enquiries once Formpop is connected.

What we collect and why

When the form opens, we will ask for your name, email address, enquiry type, message and acknowledgement of this privacy notice. A phone number and organisation name are optional. We use these details to respond to your expression of interest, discuss relevant activities or partnerships and plan our programs. An enquiry is not a booking or a subscription to marketing.

Please share only what is needed for us to respond. Do not include medical conditions, health records or other sensitive information. Adults should contact us on behalf of children without including children’s personal details.

Formpop and website services

Enquiries will be processed and stored through Formpop, including its submission protection and email notification services. Leafing Well’s website does not operate a separate enquiry database or email delivery system. Authorised people developing Leafing Well will use the details to follow up with you.

Cloudflare hosts this website. Hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and security checks to deliver and protect the service. Once enabled, Formpop’s spam protection may use Cloudflare Turnstile.

Service providers may process information outside Australia. Provider locations and retention arrangements for the live form are still being confirmed; this policy will be updated before enquiries open. We do not sell enquiry information.

Keeping information safe

We aim to limit access to people who need it to respond to enquiries and develop the initiative. We will keep enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and any applicable obligations, then delete or de-identify it. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics and does not send enquiry details to analytics services or browser logs. Security services used by the live form may use technical storage needed for their operation.

Your choices and contacting us

You can choose not to enquire or leave optional fields blank. Once enquiries open, you can use our enquiry form to request access to, correction or deletion of information you have provided, withdraw an expression of interest or raise a privacy concern. If you have already received a response from us, you can also reply directly to it. We will respond as reasonably practicable.

While the form is disabled, there is no online contact channel or enquiry collection through this website. A verified privacy contact will be added before collection begins.

Updates

We may update this notice as the initiative and its services develop. The current version will be published here.
